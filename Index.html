Gmail organizer

The problem
A crowded inbox buries the emails that matter, like receipts, job replies and order updates.
What I built
It creates six labels once: Finance, Jobs, Shopping, Tech, Social and Newsletters. It then reads each unlabeled email's sender, subject and preview, matches them against my keyword rules, and applies the right Gmail label. It handles up to 500 emails per run and retries a failed Gmail call up to three times.
What it does for you
Mail is sorted by topic, so finding a receipt or a job reply takes seconds. Emails that match no rule are left untouched, or go to an Unsorted label if you add one.

Try the sorting rules

Type an email subject or sender. The rules below are the ones in the workflow, checked in order, and the first match wins.

Facebook Messenger chatbot

The problem
Customers message a business page with the same questions again and again, and they expect a quick answer.
What I built
A chatbot connected to a Facebook page. When a customer sends a text message, the workflow reads the business's Google Doc, which works as its knowledge base, and an AI agent writes the reply. The reply goes back to the customer in Messenger, and the agent remembers the conversation. To change an answer, you edit the Google Doc. No code needed.
What it does for you
Customers get an answer without waiting for someone to be online. The bot answers only from your document, says so when it does not know, and points the customer to a team member.
